# Review checklist

Review in this order: central claim and novelty; study design and controls;
data and methods; statistics and uncertainty; figures and tables; reporting,
ethics, and reproducibility; then writing and presentation.

Record each finding with `id`, `severity`, `location`, `evidence`, `consequence`,
`request`, and optional `confidence`.
Use `fatal`, `major`, `minor`, or `suggestion` for severity. A checklist is a
triage aid, not proof of a defect.
