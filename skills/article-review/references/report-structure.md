# Review report structure

1. Review setup: input scope, target venue if supplied, and assessment boundary.
2. Shared fact base: central claim, evidence, methods, significance, and limitations.
3. Overall assessment: what is established and what is not.
4. Major findings: prioritized, evidence-grounded, and actionable.
5. Minor findings: clarity, reporting, and presentation corrections.
6. Strengths and likely readership.
7. Synthesis: highest-leverage revisions and missing materials affecting confidence.

For a bounded excerpt, omit axes that cannot be assessed and say so explicitly.
