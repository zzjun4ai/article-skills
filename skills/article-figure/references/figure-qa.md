# Final figure QA

## Scientific and data integrity

- [ ] The title/legend claim matches the panels and no panel is decorative.
- [ ] Every plotted value is reproducible from source data and a recorded script.
- [ ] Units, baselines, denominators, normalization, exclusions, and exact n are clear.
- [ ] Replicate type, uncertainty definition, tests, and multiple-testing treatment are reported.
- [ ] Image edits, crops, splices, pseudocolour, and scale bars are valid and documented.

## Visual and accessibility

- [ ] Panel labels, type, lines, ticks, symbols, and axes are legible at final size.
- [ ] Typography and numerical precision are consistent; no labels clip or overlap.
- [ ] Colour is meaningful, CVD-aware, and interpretable in grayscale.
- [ ] A legend has been replaced by direct labels where that is clearer.
- [ ] The vector master retains editable text/paths; raster panels retain effective resolution.

## Delivery bundle

- [ ] Matplotlib source code/project and locked dependency/environment record.
- [ ] Completed `figure-manifest.yaml`.
- [ ] Editable vector master and any required journal submission derivative.
- [ ] Source-data table plus data dictionary/panel map.
- [ ] Final legend and a short image-integrity/provenance note where relevant.
