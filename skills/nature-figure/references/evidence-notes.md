# Evidence notes for this skill

## Supplied colour reference

Crameri, F., Shephard, G. E. and Heron, P. J. *The misuse of colour in science
communication*. Nature Communications 11, 5444 (2020),
doi:10.1038/s41467-020-19160-7. The skill derives its no-rainbow, perceptual-order,
red-green caution, colour-vision-deficiency, and grayscale checks from this source.

## Supplied formatting reference

The local PDF whose filename says “Moore et al. (2011) BRISQ” has embedded metadata
and content identifying it as *Brief guide for submission to Nature Communications*,
revised 3 May 2019. Its verifiable figure recommendations are RGB images at 300 dpi
or higher, Arial/Helvetica, 5–8 pt text at final size, colour-safe palettes, avoiding
rainbow, and editable vector formats. They are recorded as a baseline only because
requirements can change by journal and article type.
