# Claim-evidence audit

For each consequential claim record:

- claim text and section;
- supporting result, figure, table, method, or verified citation;
- evidence strength and uncertainty;
- scope boundary, population, condition, and time frame;
- whether wording is associative, predictive, mechanistic, or causal.

Downgrade verbs when evidence is weaker than wording. `Associated with` is not
`caused`, and one dataset does not establish universality. Missing support becomes an
explicit author-input placeholder, not polished filler.
