# Section playbooks

Read only the requested section.

- `title` — name the system, phenomenon, or contribution precisely; avoid unsupported
  superlatives.
- `abstract` — context, gap, approach, principal result with evidence, and bounded
  implication. Do not introduce facts absent from the manuscript.
- `introduction` — establish the problem and gap, position prior work, then state the
  contribution and article roadmap.
- `methods` — make procedures reproducible: materials/data, design, parameters,
  controls, statistics, software, and availability.
- `results` — lead with the question, report observations and quantitative evidence,
  and separate interpretation.
- `discussion` — explain meaning, compare with prior evidence, expose limitations, and
  bound generalization.
- `conclusion` — synthesize what the evidence establishes; do not repeat the abstract or
  add new results.
