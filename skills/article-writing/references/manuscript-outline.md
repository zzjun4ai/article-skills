# Manuscript outline

Start with the research question and contribution, then map each major claim
to results, figures, tables, methods, and citations. A practical outline is
`Introduction`, `Results`, `Discussion`, `Methods`, and `Data availability`,
adapted to the target journal.

Before drafting, list unresolved inputs rather than filling them with prose.
The companion checker only detects common reference tokens; it does not verify
that the referenced figure, table, or citation exists.
